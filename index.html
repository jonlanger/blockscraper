How to build

  • Open a style, choose a colorway, then pick a block.
  • Click a plot or block face to place. Shift+click fills the plot · Ctrl/⌘+Shift+click fills the whole city block.
  • Buildings join automatically when blocks touch — build across plots and streets, as wide and as deep as you like.
  • Streets don't stop a building: build on one to close that stretch (traffic turns back), or keep it open and span upper floors over it from a building beside it.
  • Double-click (or Select tool) chooses a building · Tab cycles · Z frames it · O focuses on it alone.
  • Alt+click copies a block's type and style · R stacks the top floor.
  • Touch: drag to orbit, pinch to zoom, two-finger drag to pan, tap to build. Use Fill in the blocks panel for plot, city block, floor or building fills.
  • Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes an edit · Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Z (or Ctrl+Y) redoes it.
  • Q / E build level (any depth) · PgUp / PgDn slice floors · F cutaway · U underground · N city view.

Rules

  • Upper floors need a Grand Lobby and an Elevator Core column from the street.
  • Each Foundation Pile lets a building rise 3 more floors.
  • Subway platforms (B2) and bus bays touch an east–west street; rail platforms (B4) touch the red rail street.
  • Map blocks shape empty plots: paint land cover or water, and raise, lower or level the ground with the brush. Buildings need dry, level land.
  • Land map blocks grow the ground out past the map edge; Streets & paths add roads, bike paths, boulevards and parking — buildings open onto them.
  • Facade ornaments go on a wall: click a face for one wall, the roof of a block for all its walls, Shift+click for the whole floor, Ctrl/⌘+Shift+click for the whole building. Stacked columns, buttresses and oriels join into one. Alt+click picks up an ornament.
  • Skybridges hang from a building beside them — no block needed below. Structures & Sculpture also has loggias, statues and monuments.
  • Entries & Terraces dress a street frontage: café terraces, stoops, grand stairs, porticos and arcades take the chosen style.
  • Parks and scenic terrain give nearby buildings +10% income. Fill a whole plot with one park type to get its full layout — basins, parterres and piazzas grow with the space.
  • Some block combinations hide easter eggs .
